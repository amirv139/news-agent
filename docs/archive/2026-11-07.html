GTHA Manufacturers Win Contracts to Build Small Modular Reactors

The Ontario government has awarded contracts to manufacturers in the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area (GTHA) to build the G7's first Small Modular Reactor. Additional local manufacturers have also secured contracts to supply the Darlington Nuclear Plant.

This provides massive, long-term engineering and manufacturing work directly within our regional supply chain.
